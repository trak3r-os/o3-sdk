# Antares Auto-Tune patent, US 5,973,252

Researched 2026-11-01. Not legal advice.

- Fact: US 5,973,252, "Pitch detection and intonation correction apparatus and method", inventor Harold A. Hildebrand, priority 1997-10-27, filed 1998-10-15, granted 1999-10-26; Google Patents gives an anticipated expiration of **2018-10-14** and labels its status "Expired (lifetime)" as an assumption, not a legal conclusion (https://patents.google.com/patent/US5973252A/en).
- First claim (paraphrase): sample a waveform, compute its autocorrelation at zero lag and at a lag L, take the smallest L that minimises their difference as the period. The abstract adds resampling toward a target pitch with smoothing and optional vibrato.
- Reading: it has expired, so it does not block an autocorrelation-based detector or a retuner built today. Other patents on pitch correction were not searched; one line about this patent is all that was asked.
- Licence class: not code; no licence question.

Source: https://patents.google.com/patent/US5973252A/en
